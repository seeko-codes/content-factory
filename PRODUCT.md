## Platform

web

## Users

The channel owner records narration on this Mac. Agents produce, review, publish and analyze the videos.

## Product Purpose

Produce useful videos at volume and improve from real audience results. The current campaign serves SAT students; the future app is broader. Start with one Instagram account, @ultralearns, with 50-100 daily posts as the operating target. More accounts are an acceptable future option.

## Operating Context

The owner confirmed a Mac microphone flow with the script and a record button. Narration imports rebuild captions and animation cues. Instagram login uses the dedicated Chrome for Testing profile. See README.md for commands and research/instagram-strategy.md for the campaign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve raw human recordings and use the speaker's natural delivery. Do not replace human narration with generated audio unintentionally. Current campaign videos have no CTAs. QUALITY.md governs each finished video. The logic lives in LOGIC.md; the concepts and their details are the seven channel folders in research/channels/. Model their successful source clips before varying them. Use Kun Chen's actual backpass tool for instruction learning.

## Evidence on Hand

Scripts and working audio imports exist locally. Reference dossiers describe observed videos and their limitations. Published-result optimization requires observed Instagram metrics; views do not establish future app conversion.
