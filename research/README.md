# Content and research

## Logic, concepts and details

The **logic layer** is root-level [LOGIC.md](../LOGIC.md): read it first, agree on the intended viewer experience with the owner through [the human decision checkpoints](../README.md#human-decision-checkpoints), then select a channel. It contains the underlying principles of making videos, grounded in the DMScales/dmstrategyy studies and supported by the evidence cited there.

The **concepts layer** is [the seven researched channels](channels/README.md). Each creator has its own high-detail folder. The **details layer** lives inside those folders: clip dossiers and reconstructions retain object inventories, layouts, labels, mathematical state changes, timings, transitions and endings. [Ubilum](channels/ubilumapp/README.md) retains its complete multi-audit dossier. Choose a channel and exact clip, then model its actual execution.

The [discovery archive](discovery/README.md) preserves the wider channel-by-channel research. General science, handwritten lessons, live coding, longform-only and unverified candidates are outside the active gallery. The earlier aggregate reports and catalogue below remain historical discovery evidence, with their original scope and limitations.

## Current work

The [Instagram SAT campaign strategy](instagram-strategy.md) starts with close models of successful reference clips from the channel dossiers, establishes our baseline, then tests variations. It covers volume, audience and the weekly learning loop for `@ultralearns`. The app is broader; this campaign focuses on SAT prep with no launch CTA. Cadence and experimental allocations remain proposals.

The active slate is [20 revised SAT math video briefs for October 29, 2026](today-2026-10-29-v2.md), also available as [CSV](today-2026-10-29-v2.csv). Each connects a concrete visual construction and bounded hook to reasoning, a nearby transfer problem, accuracy conditions, reference evidence and prior lineage. These are briefs, not finished videos. Rank is editorial priority, not a view forecast or posting commitment. The [first version](today-2026-10-29.md) and its [CSV](today-2026-10-29.csv) remain preserved.

The finished-output authority is [QUALITY.md](../QUALITY.md). [STYLE.md](../STYLE.md) governs this series' execution. For production commands, use [README.md](../README.md).

## Earlier material: idea banks and historical research

Preserve these sources for discovery and lineage. Their old priorities, owner allocations, schedules, frequency estimates and expected-performance claims do not override the current slate or quality bar. Recheck any factual claim before using it in a video. An existing video is not automatically an approved quality exemplar.

| File | Use |

| [Posting calendar](posting-calendar.md) | Earlier seasonal scheduling proposal. Verify current dates before using urgency. |
| [Viral SAT research](viral-sat-research.md) | Examples to investigate; causal explanations and predicted view ranges are hypotheses, not established effects. |

When moving beyond SAT math, retain the general output bar and define the new audience's intended payoff and subject-specific accuracy criteria. Do not carry SAT promises, branding copy, or teaching assumptions into another content type by accident.
