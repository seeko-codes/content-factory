## Access and identity limits

Public HTTP and yt-dlp were used without login or cookies. Current blackpenredpen short listings included members-only videos; two attempted public watch-page extractions explicitly returned membership gates. No content was obtained through those gates. The channel remains a verified discovery reference, and those candidates must not be described as visually reviewed. A Planet Nutshell subtitle request returned HTTP 429; a visual-only media request was made separately, with no subtitle bypass.

The seed aliases `zmaths`, `jmaths`, `mathyamin`, `visualmaths_`, `mathanimation`, `mathvisualized`, `MindYourMath`, and `FortheLoveofMath` were not verified as relevant, inspectable creators in this run. Failed or empty YouTube tab retrieval is not evidence that an Instagram account does not exist. `@3DMath` resolved to a three-subscriber account named larry ortiz with one upload and was excluded as an identity mismatch. `@MathQueen` resolved to a 15-subscriber Hindi school-exam account, which does not establish the intended creator. `@mathsketch` resolved to a tiny geometry-puzzle account with one short but insufficient identity and visual evidence to include here. `@EquationLab` returned a two-view introduction, not a verified mathematical-animation catalogue. These remain leads, not invented handles or styles.

Mathemanim is included despite its small size: its first-party description identifies host Piyush and mathematical animations made with Manim, and the visible catalogue contains geometry/trigonometry/graphing videos. Small audience is not a reason to erase a distinct visual reference. Alan Becker, Cristóbal Vila and Planet Nutshell were discovered through actual first-party mathematical-video search results; their wider catalogues contain other work and are not represented as math-only channels.
