Observed 2026-10-29. This is a discovery catalogue, not a set of proven causes or a verified worldwide top ranking. Instagram rows preserve all twelve public loaded reels per profile, including lower performers. YouTube rows retain math/animation candidates screened from public official Shorts-tab titles, including low-view comparators. Most rows have not been watched. Unknown titles, dates, durations, likes and channel IDs are blank. Empty titles are deliberate, not invented from thumbnails.

## Source precision and coverage

Views and channel size are observed at the same date. Current followers/subscribers are not followers at upload, unique viewers, impressions, or a counterfactual performance baseline. Ratios are discovery signals only. Public K/M counters are rounded; CSV numbers are nominal expansions. YouTube flat-tab extraction may similarly expose rounded counts. No authenticated access, cookies, login bypass, or third-party estimates were used.

The channel follower counts and reel/Shorts counts come from the first-party profile pages and public official channel Shorts tabs. Public pages were opened in background visible dedicated Chrome for Testing; yt-dlp read public Shorts listings without authentication. Search results were used only to discover handles, never as the metric authority. Title-screening establishes candidacy, not that a video actually contains animation or a sound mathematical explanation. Adjacent physics/computing candidates are labeled in the concept column where recognized; filter those before treating the dataset as a pure math sample.

Instagram captions/titles, dates, and playback were generally unavailable behind a sign-up modal. The loaded twelve-tile grid is incomplete and may include pinned posts. It is not an all-time ranking. Four Ubilum labels are cross-referenced to the existing primary-source audit in [reference-asset-audit.md](reference-asset-audit.md); no new motion review of those videos is claimed. The HelloMath winner caption and dated og:description were directly observed on its official individual reel; its visible like counter was 127.9K, while the page description rounded it to 128K. The CSV retains 127900 as a nominal expansion. Its source date was July 17, 2026; its caption does not establish the exact animation.

## Coverage gaps and interpretation limits

- 685 candidates across ten channels/accounts: 497 CodeMatrix, 93 Zak's Lab, 19 Quanta, four Faculty of Khan and 72 Instagram. This count must never be presented as 685 watched or confirmed math animations.
- CodeMatrix's full public Shorts listing returned 831 entries. Its topic mix includes mental arithmetic, equations, matrix fact lists, geometry puzzles, formula collections and occasional physics, besides animation. The retained 497 match explicit math/visual/animation/construction terms in their titles; that screen still admits unverified examples and some adjacent material. Generic fact lists and most unrelated entries were excluded. A creator's title is not independent validation of a claim such as a shortcut's generality or an exam statistic.
- Zak's Lab includes adjacent wave/phasor/orbit physics. Quanta includes adjacent computing and mathematical physics. The concept-candidate field flags recognized adjacency, but title-based classification can be incomplete. Exclude adjacent examples from any strict core math-animation summary, or verify the individual video before including it.
- The Instagram channel bios identify math animation, but a bio does not establish each reel's format or subject. Most Instagram titles and individual dates are intentionally blank. The twelve exposed tiles per account are not a complete archive, and pinned posts may be present.
- Mathemaniac's public channel did not expose a Shorts tab in this check. Math With Bad Drawings and Planet Nutshell were considered adjacent leads but not counted without a verified relevant accessible Shorts listing. No TikTok catalogue was successfully validated in this pass. This is an access and selection gap, not evidence that those creators or that platform lack successful examples.
- No retention, completion rate, impressions, shares, saves, traffic sources, posting-time controls, historical followers or genuine counterfactuals were available. Do not turn the descriptive patterns into guaranteed success/failure rules.
- Counts are a dated snapshot, not a durable leaderboard. Current subscriber ratios are useful triage. Same-channel view distributions and individual content inspection are stronger next steps than copying a surface feature of a winner.
