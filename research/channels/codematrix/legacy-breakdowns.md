## Visual comparison: one winner and one lower-view video

The `watch` preparation helper downloaded two publicly accessible YouTube Shorts into temporary storage. No authentication was used. No scene changes were detected, so the helper selected the first frame. Each first frame plus four additional distributed stills (approximately 2, 6, 10 and 14 seconds) was visually inspected. Subtitles were unavailable, and Whisper was unavailable; neither audio nor full playback was reviewed. These observations establish sampled visual content only. Temporary research media are outside the repository, with no retained third-party video assets in Git.

The age difference is material. So are unobserved distribution, traffic source, viewer retention, historical channel size, music, replays, posting context and audience. Two clips are not a controlled comparison. The useful transfer to SAT is to make the mathematical correspondence inspectable: a point on a line tied to its rise/run, or square pieces tied to algebraic terms. A generic spinning shape cannot substitute for an inference the student needs to see. Conversely, the Fourier video's broad title and small formulas show that a high-performing example need not satisfy every educational criterion in our output bar. Reach and educational value are separate outcomes.
