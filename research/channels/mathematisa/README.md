# Mathematisa | Math Animations

[@mathematisa](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reels/), Instagram. Current public audience: **158,000**, observed **2026-10-29**, from the existing first-party profile observation. This is rounded current audience, not audience when the reels were published.

Five original portrait reels are now visually inspected across their complete durations. The review uses one-second samples throughout, half-second samples for four reels, and quarter-second samples for the odd-number dissection. Native video dimensions and durations were measured from the downloaded public media. This is a dense sampled visual audit; audio quality, musical synchronization and exact easing were not measured.

The best mathematical construction reference here is [odd-number dissection](replays/odd-sum.md): equal unit tiles change from centered odd-height columns into a square. Its individual view count is unavailable. The [nonlinear-mapping reel](replays/nonlinear-maps.md) is the stronger verified distribution reference: **855,000 rounded grid views**, approximately **5.41 times** the current audience, with three explicitly labeled maps deforming coordinate grids. It is mathematically substantive but visually dense enough to cover its own text. Those limitations are recorded, not polished out of the evidence.

The [function montage](replays/function-montage.md) has 1.2 million rounded grid views and four repeated plots, rather than four independent comparisons. The [two greeting reels](replays/greetings.md), with 2.5 million and 2.2 million rounded grid views, preserve a different within-channel format: symbolic calculus becomes an Eid greeting. Their distribution does not establish the learning effectiveness of the geometric explanations.
