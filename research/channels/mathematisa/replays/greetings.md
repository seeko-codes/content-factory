## Relation and limits of the pair

The shared layout makes the two greetings a close variant comparison: same black stage, two-line salutation, central white symbolic reduction and small bottom wordmark; different calculus entry point, palette, duration and final artwork. The Fitr sequence uses a trigonometric identity and ln antiderivative. The Adha sequence uses a special-function identity, series recognition and exponential antiderivative. Calling both simply equation animations would discard the operation responsible for each reveal.

Neither clip is an introductory calculus lesson. The viewer is expected to recognize large symbolic changes quickly, and the source supplies no error explanation, numeric check or captioned theorem condition. They are useful references for a short puzzle-to-message resolution and for how a mathematical object can become the emotional ending. Their exceptionally large observed distribution remains descriptive and seasonal; it is not evidence that this format teaches transformations or dissections better than the channel's other examples.
