## Mathematical interpretation and replay limits

The order links several familiar families, but does not build one argument across them. It is a rapid catalogue of shape/formula relationships. The four duplicates do not add four independent observations, accuracy checks or side-by-side function comparisons. A reconstruction that puts a different function into each quadrant would be a new format, not this source layout.

The approximation sign matters. Finite Maclaurin polynomials are not globally equal to sine, cosine or the exponential function. Local accuracy depends on degree and the x-range. Neither numerical axes nor an error threshold is supplied, so apparent closeness in the cropped plot cannot certify approximation quality. Interpolated curve frames may temporarily correspond to neither adjoining formula; they are motion, not extra theorems.

The implicit cases have different topology and real-domain behavior. Even-power x^m+y^m=1 gives a closed superellipse for positive even m; odd-power sums need not be closed. The alternating visual families should not be described as one continuously shrinking closed curve. The heart equation is a new implicit relation, not the n-to-infinity limit of the preceding family. These distinctions preserve the source's actual variety while preventing a fast montage from becoming an invented mathematical proof.
