# Sources, method and unknowns

Observed 2026-10-29. Identity, 158,000 rounded current followers and twelve initial reel tiles come from the existing [public profile/reels observation](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reels/). [grid-observation.md](grid-observation.md) preserves that original scope and its login-gate limit. The twelve tiles are incomplete and potentially pinned; their median is not a complete channel baseline.

Five individual public reels were accessible through unauthenticated yt-dlp: [odd sum](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUlqKxGE1UO/), [nonlinear transformations](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reel/DVJCut-iSB9/), [graph montage](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reel/DVTu-3CiXoS/), [Fitr greeting](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reel/DWEoj8Iif4G/) and [Adha greeting](https://www.instagram.com/mathematisa/reel/DY0FlG6zxc5/). Original streams, audio tracks and watch metadata were downloaded temporarily. No cookies, credentials, login or access bypass were used.

Every second across each complete reel was inspected, including a final-frame sample. Additional samples cover each half-second for the two greetings, graph montage and nonlinear transformation, and each quarter-second for the odd-sum dissection. The replay timestamps identify observed instants or bounded intervals, not recovered keyframes. Approximate transition boundaries are limited by 0.25-0.5-second sampling. Formula text was read from stable states rather than treated as correct during morphs. Source captions supply context but do not override a visibly different construction.

FFprobe measured video dimensions, video stream durations and container durations. Audio can extend the container a few hundredths of a second beyond the last video frame. The CSV retains both durations. These are measured media properties, not an Instagram display rounded to whole seconds. All five sources are portrait, four delivered at 1080x1920 and one at 360x640. The lower-resolution Adha copy is sufficient for state identification but cannot establish original upload resolution or fine edge quality.

The extractor calls all five items `Video by mathematisa`; that is a generated fallback, not a verified native title. Descriptive titles in this module are explicitly editorial labels based on visible content. Publication dates are from the public watch metadata. Native descriptions were available for four items; the Fitr description was absent. No exact view counts were returned. The four existing grid figures, 2.5M, 2.2M, 1.2M and 855K, remain rounded display observations, never rewritten as exact counts. The separately supplied odd-sum source has no observed views in this pass.

Like counts were returned but are not a robust cross-reel comparison: the Adha response gave 3 while the preserved grid observation gave 2.2M views. That discrepancy cannot be resolved from the available evidence. [videos.csv](videos.csv) retains the returned counts with an explicit caution instead of explaining them away. A view/current-follower ratio uses the 2026-10-29 rounded current follower denominator. It is not reach among followers, audience at upload, unique viewers, or a causal explanation of success.

The dedicated visible Chrome session was requested with background-only navigation. It twice returned `BRIDGE_NOT_READY` because its attached CDP target had gone away. No second Chrome copy was launched, and no native click or focus-taking action was used. Public media retrieval succeeded independently. The user's foreground app remained Ghostty in the focus checks.

Audio tracks exist, but music, speech intelligibility, sound effects and beat synchronization were not auditioned. No transcript or narration claims are made. Exact fonts, software, source code, easing curves, object hierarchy and physical camera behavior remain unknown. The replay specs are visual and mathematical descriptions, not recovered authoring files. Original video and extraction metadata remain temporary outside the repository. At the user's request, twenty-four local JPEG evidence images are retained under ignored `out/channel-references/mathematisa/`: twelve contact sheets, five exact last frames, one ending comparison sheet and six native-resolution detail frames. These images are excluded from Git. Their dossier links work in this workspace; remote readers must retrieve the public sources. The [ending comparison](../../../out/channel-references/mathematisa/all-five-endings.jpg) verifies the actual final video frames of all five clips, rather than only an approximate last seek.
