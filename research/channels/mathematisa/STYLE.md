## What fits and what breaks this creator's reference

The dissection works when every tile has equal area, the moved piece stays the same piece, and the sum remains connected to the number of columns. It breaks if added squares are decorative, if the piece changes tile count during rotation, or if the final n labels have no connection to the constructed width. In the source, a moving piece briefly touches or crosses the left frame edge; preserve that as an observation while recognizing its cost to following the proof.

The mapping variant works as a compact preview of unfamiliar nonlinear behavior. It breaks as an explanation if the viewer cannot identify the input grid, match each formula to its result, or distinguish a transition from the endpoint map. Formula/curve overlap and unlabeled input extent already limit this example's precision. A visually elaborate grid is not proof of injectivity or area preservation.

The greeting variant works as a symbolic puzzle and celebration for viewers who already recognize the calculus identities. Literal holiday words are not numerical constants. Reusing the reveal as a rigorous exercise requires defining formal parameters and domains, rather than pretending the seasonal typography is a numeric evaluation. High seasonal reach is evidence of observed distribution only; its cause remains unknown.
