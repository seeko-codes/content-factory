# Concepts layer: the seven researched channels

These seven channels are the concepts layer: concrete manifestations of the [DMScales-derived logic](../../LOGIC.md). Each folder preserves one creator's visual language and complete inspected constructions. The details layer lives inside those same folders: clip dossiers, timestamped audits, layouts, mathematical reconstructions, transitions, endings and evidence limits. Open the relevant channel README, then its specific clip studies. Preserve their depth and independence; this index routes to them without blending their styles.

Admission is based on the inspected video: mathematical objects must carry the action through animation, and the format must fit a Reel or Short. A channel's reputation alone does not admit its unreviewed uploads. Talking heads, handwriting lessons, live coding, general science and longform-only references are outside this active set. Mixed channels contribute only the named qualifying clips. Lower-performing or incomplete clips are retained as explicitly marked within-channel contrasts.

GraduateML is a user-nominated quality reference with strong observed engagement. Its public review did not establish Reel view counts, so it is not presented as an independently verified view-count super-performer. All ratios use cumulative views divided by the current channel audience, not audience at upload; platform counts and rounded Instagram grid values are not directly comparable. No ratio demonstrates causation, retention, learning or repeatability.

The dossiers preserve layout, asset inventory, color roles, text hierarchy, scene progression, mathematical invariants, transitions, holds and endings. They distinguish delivered-frame observations from independently authored reconstruction math and unknown source details. Sampled inspection is not continuous playback, and an unheard soundtrack stays unverified. Exact project files, proprietary fonts and hidden production settings cannot be inferred from a finished video.

The much wider collection is retained in [discovery](../discovery/README.md), with original per-channel research and bounded metadata. It is outside the approved reference set. The original [1,401-row catalogue](../math-animation-catalogue.csv) is discovery evidence, not 1,401 animation exemplars. Use each active folder's `videos.csv` or explicit capture index for reviewed sources; `catalogue-candidates.csv` preserves broader unreviewed listings.
