## What the format comparisons actually support

The current four known course-oriented topics occupy the top four positions **within the loaded 12**. In the older nine-topic selection, three course overviews occupy three of the top four like counts. This repeated local signal makes bounded overviews a sensible creative candidate. It does not identify a channel-wide winner: the topics were deliberately selected for analysis, eight current subjects lack ID-level classification, and upload age, impression supply, audience prerequisites and distribution history are mostly missing.

Useful comparisons describe a construction before judging its popularity. Calc III grows a familiar curve into surfaces, slices, accumulation and fields while retaining recognizable referents. All-calculus organizes chapters around local simplification. Sine links a rotating point and graph to its rate of change. Calc II preserves a quantitative area through substitution and contrasts a slower-decaying curve. These are inspectable design distinctions in the [asset audit](../../reference-asset-audit.md), not explanations derived merely from “the winner won.”

For SAT, compare **orientation**, **single-relation explanation**, **notation demystification** and **counterexample** as separate audience promises. An overview should leave a useful map; a single-skill lesson should leave a decisive reason and recognition cue. A narrow slope lesson can offer more usable learning than a widely viewed survey. Public counters reveal neither completion, learning, trust, transfer, watch-time distribution nor whether a paid or organic mechanism supplied exposure.

The legacy claim that 3D beats 2D is not established: the strong sine example is two-dimensional, and the lower-count integral, research problem and exponential topics differ in much more than depth. Likewise “scary symbol” appears in both gradient and integral with very different likes. A question, sophisticated motion or consistent typography may help craft without being sufficient for reach. Our principles can generate testable predictions about comprehension and continuity; this observational sample cannot validate every principle as a law of distribution.

“99% get this wrong” is especially unsupported here. No complete inventory of such titles, measured error rate, impressions or matched outcomes exists in these observations. Its absence from verified examples does not prove universal failure; the scarcity of evidence cannot justify adopting a false statistic either. For our SAT output, an unverified error-rate claim fails the trust bar regardless of possible reach. Use an actual tempting mistake with a checked correction.

## Source distinctions carried into the style guide

[STYLE.md](../../../STYLE.md) now distinguishes source observations from our production choices. Preserve the following distinctions when using the reference:

- The typed `ultralearn` comment CTA, SAT-prep wording and Ultralearn end card are **our marketing choices**. The source profile and inspected endings identify Ubilum. Neither a mandatory identical CTA nor our keyword is established as its performance mechanism.
- One blue is our useful default, not an absolute source limit: the linear-algebra comparison uses blue and muted violet with corresponding multipliers. Preserve semantic contrast rather than copying an inaccurate prohibition.
- A map-first SAT hook is the user's requested adaptation. The inspected Calc III opening has one curve; all-calculus initially shows a surface/title composition. Neither establishes a winning dashboard opening.
- A return to the initial picture in a screen recording may be replay. It does not prove a seamless original edit, exact loop boundary or loop-driven performance.
- Measured font, motion, pitch and timing observations apply to their reviewed samples. Silent Mac recordings cannot establish narration or absence of music across the account. House defaults can be decisive without being mislabeled as universal source measurements.

The practical output target is an honest promise, traceable object relationships, legible mathematical evidence and an earned recap. Ubilum supplies specific constructions worth adapting. Its public counts select examples to study; they do not approve our finished videos or guarantee the next result.
