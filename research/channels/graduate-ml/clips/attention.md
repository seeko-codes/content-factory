## Mathematics and rhetorical boundaries

The scaling expression is the query-key dot product divided by the square root of key dimension. The example contrasts dimension six and sixty-four with blue score/gold softmax bars. The clip does not supply the underlying query/key vectors, their variance assumptions or numerical logits. Consequently, the bars are a conceptual demonstration of distribution/normalization behavior, not a reproducible quantitative derivation from supplied vectors.

The parallel relation rows illustrate the idea that multiple heads can attend to different token relationships. Labels such as grammar, nearby words and meaning make those distinctions readable, but do not establish that specific trained-model heads always have those clean semantic roles. A faithful source record retains the labels as the creator's illustration and marks the generalization limit. The grid is a causal mask: future token access is blocked relative to current position. It cannot be recreated as a static triangle without consistent token axes and the intended direction of temporal access.

The pyramid's skill percentages are not mathematical consequences of understanding those mechanisms. They are an engagement structure. There is no inspected survey, skill-distribution study or benchmark supporting those percentile claims. The ending links entry into the top tier to following and visiting the creator domain; this is marketing, not evidence that following changes engineering competence. Keeping that distinction explicit preserves both the actual content and its limits.
