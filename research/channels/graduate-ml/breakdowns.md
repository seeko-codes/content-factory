# GraduateML: clip-by-clip reconstruction records

All four records concern actual publicly downloaded first-party media. The native Instagram reels are the primary sources for robotics. Every two-second sample and late frame was inspected, with selected additional higher-detail frames. These are dense state records rather than continuous full-motion playback. Local ASR supplied complete temporary narration text, used here as paraphrased timed speech maps; audio production remains unaudited.

- [MPC](clips/mpc.md): 20 states, robot rig/world/trajectory/cost/selection/execute/replan/rewind sequence, formula caveats and asset dependencies.
- [Drone training question](clips/drone-question.md): 34 states, simulation-to-real mismatch, history encoder, latent/controller pipeline, four objective cards, final choice grid and engagement ending.
- [CNN](clips/cnn.md): 20 states, pixel-art sword and numeric patch, exact observed filter, scanning and feature-map layout, channel/layer stacks and class/zoom loop.
- [Attention](clips/attention.md): 25 states, repeated sentence tokens, query/key/value symbols, scaling and softmax, relation rows, causal-mask grid, hierarchy returns and bright CTA.

Do not treat the two YouTube records as unusually viral uploads: their observed view counts are modest. The native Instagram reels have high observed like counts, but views were unavailable. All source metrics and publication dates remain platform-specific in [videos.csv](videos.csv).
