# Sources and methods

- [First-party channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC0cd_-e49hZpWLH3UIwoWRA), observed 2026-10-29; identity, description, public displayed subscribers and bounded tab listing extracted without cookies or login using yt-dlp.
- [Retrieved tab](https://www.youtube.com/@ProfessorDaveExplains/shorts); flat-tab counts may be rounded, and missing views or dates remain blank.
- Every candidate has a first-party watch URL in [videos.csv](videos.csv). An individual watch-page info extraction, when present, supplies that row's publication date, duration and current view count. These values may differ from rounded tab values.

No causal performance inference, historical subscriber denominator, complete catalogue, independent listening, or identity matching across platforms is claimed. Names proposed in the original discovery queue are not assumed to be this account. Downloaded media and subtitle files remain temporary and are not copied into this module.
