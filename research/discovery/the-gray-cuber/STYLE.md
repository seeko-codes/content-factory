# TheGrayCuber: observed execution

This channel's two samples have an immediately distinctive dark blue-purple canvas, playful heavy sans lettering, bright pink/green/cyan accents and a loose, deliberately irregular frame. [How to Say Numbers Slower](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wnjd0SLXZgs) builds a typographic comedy around arithmetic and naming rules. [hiking in the imaginary numbers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32SyhSNtGew) turns almost the whole frame into a saturated contour landscape. The palette and playful labeling connect them, but their central assets and densities differ dramatically.

Numbers Slower uses language as an object. A dentist dialogue appears at slight angles in separate white and pink blocks. Green highlights pick out the alternative descriptions and quantitative score, while purple supports intermediate notation. Large numerals and very long expressions coexist; the comedy depends on the difference between the short number and the cumbersome naming system. At 05:10 a scoring program explicitly combines letters and four times syllables. At 07:45 the output is graded with a B-minus and percentage. These are authored explanatory graphics rather than verbatim speech captions. The text's changing scale and position are visible across samples; exact easing and whether every transition uses rotation are unknown.

The imaginary-number video starts inside the colorful result rather than introducing a title card. Broad contour bands in navy, cyan, blue and lilac define large regions. Thin, luminous-looking borders occur along some transitions. Small hand-drawn-looking labels identify elliptical maps, while a yellow highlighted contour and focal markers briefly make the distance construction inspectable. Later, the landscape yields to a section roadmap and a simple complex-vector diagram on a faint grid. This switch matters: without that calmer geometry, the beautiful map would remain difficult to decode.

The latter ending becomes especially experimental. A supporter-name collage interrupts the visual exploration, and late frames return to a fractured, bright map before a plain blue final state. The Numbers Slower ending keeps the oversized dentist response behind a boxed supporter collage. That is different from a conventional clean credits slate and should be preserved as an observed variant. Neither sample shows a face-camera presenter. Subtitle excerpts can establish the topic and some terminology, but voice performance and sound design are not audited.

Useful cases are playful mathematical language experiments and contour-based explorations of complex-valued constructions. This execution would be damaged by turning every word into equally bright kinetic type, because the pink/green role distinction currently helps separate the joke from the calculation. For the contour video, removing the highlighted level, focal markers or component vector would leave an attractive but uninterpretable color field. Conversely, a dry formula-only reconstruction would lose the immersive map that the opening actually promises. Exact color-to-value mapping needs explicit explanation in any new implementation; the stills alone do not supply it.

## Evidence boundary

These are two-video, sixteen-frame-per-video sampled observations, not continuous playback. Intermediate motion is inferred only where successive states establish it. Exact timing curves, frame-accurate transitions, font identities and sound quality were not verified.
