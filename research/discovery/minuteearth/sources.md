# Sources, method and unknowns

Observed 2026-10-29. Identity, current audience and discovery entries came from [MinuteEarth's public Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/@MinuteEarth/shorts) and [Videos](https://www.youtube.com/@MinuteEarth/videos) tabs, requested with yt-dlp in a bounded sample of at most four entries per tab.

- [What makes some sunsets so spectacular?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EVGE1Mu9PTQ): watch-page metadata, temporary video download, twelve extracted visual samples, public English subtitles.
- [How to Tame Your Pet AI Dragon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1bjkZYmQefA): watch-page metadata, temporary video download, twelve extracted visual samples, public English subtitles.

The watch metadata supplies precise integer views, publication dates and durations for reviewed clips. Remaining flat-tab entries may have rounded views and missing dates/durations; blanks mean unavailable, not zero. Subscriber precision remains limited by YouTube's public rounded count. The CSV ratio divides observed views by the same-date current public audience, not the creator's audience when posted. It is not unique reach, subscriber conversion or a causal performance comparison.

Media, contact sheets and subtitle files remain temporary outside the repository. No credentials, cookies, sign-in or access bypass were used. Review claims are limited to sampled frames and available subtitle wording. Native typeface, animation software, shader details, exact easing and audio quality remain unverified. A visually convincing simulation is not independent proof of its scientific assumptions. The finite sample does not establish how often any variant appears across the channel.
