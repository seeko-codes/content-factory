# Sources and limits

Observed on 2026-10-29. Identity, description, rounded subscriber count and candidates were returned by YouTube's own public [videos tab](https://www.youtube.com/@standupmaths/videos) through yt-dlp flat-playlist extraction. Canonical [channel-ID link](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCSju5G2aFaWMqn-_0YBtq5A) establishes identity independently of the display name.

The recent tab was bounded to ten entries. An attempted popular-sort query returned the identical order and was discarded rather than described as a second sample. Therefore "highest observed" means within the catalogued recent-tab sample, never a verified all-time channel leader. Date cells stay blank when the flat tab supplies no publication date; dates for selected review videos come from their own watch-page metadata. No estimated dates were manufactured.

For selected visual reviews, publicly downloadable video-only streams were sampled at sixteen timestamped positions, including opening and late/end frames. Those are direct frame observations, not continuous playback. Caption text, when available, can corroborate terminology; it does not verify voice performance, music, loudness or exact synchronization. Exact fonts, animation software and easing are unknown unless separately cited from the creator. Public media and subtitles stayed in temporary storage, outside this repository.

Each candidate's first-party watch URL is recorded in [videos.csv](videos.csv). View counts from channel tabs are rounded; watch-page values can be more precise. Counts are observations with this collection date, not forecasts. Width/height/aspect ratio describe the downloaded review stream where available, not every upload in the channel. No cookies, login or access-control bypass was used.
