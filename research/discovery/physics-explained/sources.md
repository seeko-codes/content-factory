# Sources and method

Observed 2026-10-29 through public, unauthenticated YouTube tab metadata using yt-dlp.

- [Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PhysicsExplainedVideos)
- [Shorts tab](https://www.youtube.com/@PhysicsExplainedVideos/shorts)
- [Videos tab](https://www.youtube.com/@PhysicsExplainedVideos/videos)

At most four entries were requested from each tab. An absent Shorts result means the requested tab returned no entries in this observation, not proof that the creator has never published short videos. Audience counts are rounded public subscriber counts. Flat tab view counts may also be rounded. Dates and durations unavailable in flat metadata remain blank.

A view/current-audience ratio is descriptive: observed views divided by this observation's subscribers. It is neither reach among subscribers nor an at-upload denominator, and repeated viewers and non-subscribers are included in views. No causal success inference, complete historical coverage, exact typeface or production stack is asserted. Original linked videos remain the source of truth.
