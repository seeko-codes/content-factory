## Explanatory apparatus and asset decisions

Taking Flight builds its reveal through camera traversal. An apparent arch or hollow surface gives way to ridges, pores and differently framed folds as the view advances. The object itself is not visibly decomposed into instructional parts. The Dragon is an object inspection: the silhouette and interior change as the three-dimensional form turns, with a repeat-like sequence of orientations. This is mathematical rendering as exploration, not a solved word problem. Public titles identify Mandelbulb and power-two Julia-bulb constructions; the footage alone does not specify the iteration, escape condition or parameter set.

## Fit, failure modes and observation limits

Choose this creator for mathematical scale, self-similar geometry or a three-dimensional form where the journey itself is the spectacle. Do not pretend the dramatic fractal interior is already an explanation of a SAT relationship. A SAT brief needs visible measurements or an accompanying authored model. What breaks the inspected art is a camera path so erratic the viewer loses surface continuity, clipped highlights that erase fine detail, or unrelated giant typography over the point of interest. The apparent recurring poses of The Dragon are not proof of a seamless encoded loop. Audio, rendering code and native resolution were not audited in this visual-only pass.
