## Appearance and variants

The FFT excerpt is black with thin white serif math, green/blue accents, diagram panels and compact code-style signatures. The A* excerpt uses a deep purple field, a pale street network and yellow exploration traces, followed by a bright green final path. Later, numbered graph vertices and directed weighted edges replace the street illustration. The channel uses both a restrained algebra board and a colored graph scene; preserve that difference. These inspected references are long-form excerpts, not Shorts and not a model for the timing of a 30-second reel.

## Explanatory apparatus and asset decisions

The FFT opening contrasts utility with beauty using two framed algorithm animations, then introduces polynomial coefficients, multiplication and alternative polynomial representations. A sampled later linear graph begins to connect polynomial notation to a visual object. The A* opening starts with a map-like network, explores candidate edges and then makes the selected path clear; a later excerpt replaces this familiar setting with a small abstract weighted graph and a minimum-cost expression. This is an explicit map-to-mathematical-model transition rather than a coding screen recording.

## Fit, failure modes and observation limits

Choose Reducible for algorithms whose mathematical objects can be animated independently of implementation: graph search, coefficient collections, transforms or computational geometry. For SAT, the polynomial or network object may be useful; the entire algorithm lecture and its delayed payoff are not an appropriate short-form template. What breaks these examples is coloring explored edges identically to the final route, stripping edge weights from a cost argument, or displaying code while leaving the represented mathematical object unexplained. Only 12 frames per video were inspected: six in the opening minute and six around 182-232 seconds. The FFT is about 28 minutes and A* about 16 minutes. Whole-video pacing, endings, soundtrack and formal correctness of every algorithmic step were not audited.
