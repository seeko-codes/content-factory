# Mathologer: observed execution

The pair shows two substantial Mathologer arrangements. [Petr's Miracle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLAW5yz5O3E) is a presenter-led masterclass against a clean white field, with multicolored polygon constructions to his side and an occasional historical document or portrait. [Parity of permutations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rUiulWItECQ) begins in a room full of physical mathematical puzzles, then moves into browser-like visualizers with the presenter in a circular picture-in-picture. Keeping both matters: the channel is not adequately described as only whiteboard animation or only talking-head mathematics.

The white masterclass uses black outlines and discrete colored vertices to keep correspondences across polygons. Several small constructions are retained in a lower strip while the active example grows above. That strip functions as a comparison history, especially when examples are circled or crossed out. Complex-number coordinates and a matrix eventually sit beneath a geometric figure. Here the formal apparatus arrives as a different view of a figure that the viewer has already been asked to inspect. A portrait and 1905 source page anchor the historical claim visually, although small body text is not readable at the sampled size.

The parity video uses an interface as the mathematical stage. Pale numbered top nodes map through thin crossing lines to saturated magenta lower tiles; blue buttons and text labels expose the available operations. The inversion count and parity are readouts. A 15-puzzle grid and Rubik's-cube corner/edge permutations become alternate modes of the same interface. The circular presenter keeps a human presence without covering the main correspondence lines. The rows and labels are landscape-sized and would need redesign for a phone, especially once twenty objects appear.

Typography varies by role: the logo uses a bold green name with an orange initial badge, the masterclass has clean diagram labels and coordinate notation, and the visualizer uses compact interface text. This review identifies those roles, not exact font files. No persistent speech-caption strip appears in the samples. The physical puzzle room supplies color and texture in the newer opening; the explanatory screens return to white for maximum line contrast. Both endings visibly switch to black supporter credits with many names, rather than looping the opening object.

The useful study cases are a long geometric masterclass and an invariant explored through manipulable representations. A weak imitation would remove the interface state readouts, lose the correspondence between top and bottom nodes, or substitute unrelated puzzle footage for the actual allowed moves. Another failure would be to treat one attractive polygon as a proof of a universal theorem. The masterclass's multiple examples, explicit exclusions and later algebra are part of its execution. Speech and exact movement cadence remain unaudited; frame sequences support layout and state changes only.

## Evidence boundary

These are two-video, sixteen-frame-per-video sampled observations, not continuous playback. Intermediate motion is inferred only where successive states establish it. Exact timing curves, frame-accurate transitions, font identities and sound quality were not verified. Captions mentioned below are visible authored text; available subtitle text was used only to corroborate terms. The pair is intentionally contrasting and does not define every upload.
