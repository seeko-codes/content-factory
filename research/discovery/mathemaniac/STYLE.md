# Mathemaniac: observed execution

Both reviewed videos use an unusually spare black space in which quite dense mathematics can remain visible. Serif mathematical typesetting, modest prose labels and thin colored geometry share a largely fixed frame. Yellow emphasizes selected algebra, cyan/teal fields or conclusions, pink/purple paths and surfaces, and white supplies neutral statements. The subject-specific apparatus is not a generic graph: [the action-principle video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohrl3S2wcBU) builds curves, spanning ribbons, vector fields and flux, while [the vector-potential video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iiPt83dAz18) uses oriented triangles, tetrahedral comparisons and circulation diagrams.

The first video visibly links the integral of T minus V to a geometric account of action. A purple path does not merely move across an unrelated background: it sits in a coordinate/field construction that reappears beside the corresponding integral. Around 12:31, a purple spanning surface and a flux equation coexist. At 14:36, the diagram isolates a path-variation direction. These images are useful because the mathematics refers to orientation and the surface between two paths. Color labels and small bounding boxes keep the same variable or selected component identifiable across a derivation.

The newer vector-potential sample preserves the black/serif/color grammar but changes its explanatory instrument. Long triangular wedges carry stripes, and a yellow tetrahedron separates base-point choices. Split frames let a local construction and a comparison statement remain on screen together. Some earlier material is dimmed rather than removed, creating a memory layer. However, at 360p several labels are small; reproducing this for short vertical video would require a new composition rather than cropping the landscape frame. The technical density belongs to an extended proof with established notation.

No face-camera footage or full-sentence burned-in speech captions appears in the sampled sets. Authored text is formal mathematics, short qualifications, explanatory arrows and occasional creator notes or source screenshots. Available subtitles corroborate terminology in the newer sample; they do not establish narration delivery or audio polish. The endings include explicit watch-more/subscribe language, with the newer sample showing a supplemental-material location before an end-screen arrangement. Exact animation stack, fonts and easing are not inferred from the apparent Manim-like visual vocabulary.

The execution is worth consulting for geometric derivations whose force depends on a path, normal, orientation or integral rather than a single final formula. It fails if a diagram's orientation silently flips while the algebra stays fixed, if dimmed context becomes indistinguishable from an invalidated claim, or if local/domain conditions disappear when the visual is simplified. The newer opening explicitly qualifies the domain of the vector-potential statement; that qualification is part of the design, not expendable small print.

## Evidence boundary

These are two-video, sixteen-frame-per-video sampled observations, not continuous playback. Intermediate motion is inferred only where successive states establish it. Exact timing curves, frame-accurate transitions, font identities and sound quality were not verified. Captions mentioned below are visible authored text; available subtitle text was used only to corroborate terms. The pair is intentionally contrasting and does not define every upload.
