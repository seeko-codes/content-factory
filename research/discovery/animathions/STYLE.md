## Appearance and variants

The inspected pair consists of very small, centrally grouped geometry on a black portrait stage. White outlines and italic mathematical labels carry the objects; saturated red, blue and green provide semantic regions. Large empty black areas surround the construction. The tangent-circle Short uses a blue hatched curvilinear gap, red center triangle and green sector hatching. The Pythagorean Short uses four blue triangles, a red central square and a green outer-square overlay. There is no presenter or photographed worksheet. The source's relatively tiny labels are a real limitation on a phone, not an instruction to reproduce the same small scale.

## Explanatory apparatus and asset decisions

For the three tangent unit circles, joining their centers exposes an equilateral triangle of side two. The desired gap is the triangle less three 60-degree sectors. The colored hatching tells the viewer exactly which regions are being subtracted. In the Pythagorean example, copies of one triangle create an outer square of side a+b and an inner square of side c; the displayed area accounting separates four triangle areas from the central square. The powerful asset choice here is not a morph between unrelated figures: it is four recognizable copies of the same labeled triangle.

## Fit, failure modes and observation limits

Choose this creator for explicit colored area bookkeeping on compact diagrams. Both examples make a small mathematical apparatus sufficient; neither needs a universe map. What breaks the tangent-circle example is forgetting the equal-radius assumption, subtracting three full circles instead of sectors, or interpreting a hatched sector as the blue target. What breaks the square proof is moving triangles in ways that change a or b, or calling the middle quadrilateral a square without its angle justification. Our adaptation should enlarge the construction and labels enough to read at output size. The Pythagorean Short is 74 seconds; the last inspected sample is about 61 seconds, so its final algebra and ending are not newly verified here.
