## Fresh footage observation

The public source videos in reviewed-videos.csv were downloaded without credentials on 2026-10-29 as video-only streams, with individual public metadata. Twelve timestamped frames per clip were inspected. Short sources were sampled over the first approximately 65 seconds or their entire duration when shorter; long-form sources were sampled in the opening minute and a one-minute excerpt near three minutes. Exact sample times are in breakdowns.md. The original catalogue remains unchanged; counts can differ slightly from the earlier same-day observation. Temporary media is not part of the tracked research.
