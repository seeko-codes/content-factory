# Math-channel discovery

Current placement: these creator folders are preserved in [discovery](discovery/README.md), outside the [seven-channel active gallery](channels/README.md). The CSV records final `active_gallery` membership separately from original eligibility classifications.

Collection date: 2026-10-29. This addition verifies 23 first-party YouTube channel identities with channel-ID links, public audience counts and bounded recent-upload candidate lists. Each channel has its own folder under `research/discovery/`; see [the discovery CSV](discovery-math.csv) for exact handle, source, scope and review status. Existing gallery channels were excluded. No cross-channel style synthesis is intended here.

## Discovery and verification

Web search supplied candidate identity leads. Verification used each candidate's own public YouTube videos tab through yt-dlp, retaining the actual returned uploader handle and channel ID rather than trusting a similar name. Audience counts and channel-tab view counts are public, rounded snapshots. The recent tab was bounded to ten entries; five-video and six-video returns for Nemean and Lines That Connect are preserved without inferring coverage beyond that tab. A requested popular-sort query returned the same order as recent, so it contributes no separate population or all-time ranking.

Ten channels now have two-video sampled visual reviews, totaling twenty videos and 320 timestamped frame observations. Final review statuses and exact clips belong in their individual folders and the CSV. Selected videos are public video-only streams with sixteen timestamped frames each. Available subtitle text may corroborate terms; sound-mix and continuous-motion claims remain unverified. Media and frame sheets stay under temporary storage rather than Git.

The user subsequently narrowed the active gallery to a few fully mathematical animated portrait shorts/Instagram references with unusually strong observed reach. This judges the inspected clips, not all uploads in their channels. Thirteen remaining verified identities are discovery-only. Every creator and video CSV records eligibility and its reason.
