# Reference asset audit for sat-001

Audited 2026-10-29. Purpose: choose the visual assets for the selected second script, **All of SAT Math in 60 seconds**, using the saved reference recordings. These are proposed choices, not changes to the video. The four SAT hero diagrams should stay **2D**. Borrow the references' clear correspondence between objects, selective blue emphasis, and compressed recap.

## Proposed asset decisions

1. **One shared 2D drawing stage, four final pictures.** Use SVG geometry for the line, curves, dot-plot balance and right triangle in a circle. Keep the paper, type roles and stroke hierarchy already defined in [the theme](../src/theme.ts). No photos, raster illustrations, 3D surface, extra colors or reference-brand marks are needed. The selected script teaches no quantity that requires depth.

2. **Algebra hero: a staircase that resolves into a line.** Retain the visible start at 3, equal horizontal steps and blue rises of `+2`, followed by the ink line `y = 3 + 2x`. This is the clearest counterpart to the reference's point-plus-guide method. Keep each label adjacent to its own rise, with enough space to read it at phone size. The final thumbnail should preserve the staircase silhouette, not become an anonymous diagonal.

3. **Advanced-math hero: the same chart, visibly changing steps.** Keep the existing parabola and fixed start point. On the chapter change, clear the old `+2` staircase labels, bend the ink curve, then rebuild its signed step sequence left to right: `-2, 0, +2, +4`. Show the zero step on its horizontal run. This pins “the step grows” to the sequence across the quadratic, rather than implying that every line interval moves upward during the morph. Introduce the exponential as a lighter second curve with its own nearby formula. The underlying functions remain those in [the source definitions](../src/videos/sat-001/SatMath.tsx). This specifies the next asset pass; no video code has changed.

4. **Data hero: ten countable dots, one balance point, one ten-cell strip.** Use the existing data `[1,2,3,3,4,4,4,5,6,8]`, whose sum is 40 and mean is 4. Highlight exactly the dots at 5, 6 and 8 for `3 / 10 = 30%` ([data and readout source](../src/videos/sat-001/SatMath.tsx)). Make “above the mean” read as numerically greater than 4: the relevant dots sit to the right of 4 on a horizontal number line. A temporary guide at 4 or a restrained region cue would clarify that selection. Keep the ten-cell strip visibly linked to those three dots so it does not look like a separate unexplained chart.

5. **Geometry hero: a 3-4-5 triangle with a readable right-angle marker and diameter.** Reuse the three selected dots as moving corners, then draw the triangle and circle. Preserve a quiet circle and make the diameter blue when named. The transition from data values 5, 6 and 8 to triangle corners is a visual handoff, not a mathematical relationship. Avoid carrying those data values into side labels. Introduce `3² + 4² = 5²` only once the new side lengths are clear ([current geometry](../src/videos/sat-001/SatMath.tsx)).

6. **Opening and recap: the same four silhouettes.** Keep a compact two-by-two map as the proposed adaptation for four domains, then expand one picture at a time. Reintroduce the identical miniatures on “A line. A curve. A balance. A triangle.”, with label and question range placed consistently. The reference evidence supports recognizable reuse and compression; it does not establish a two-by-two grid as a performance ingredient. Do not add formulas and counts to every miniature if they compete with the four shapes.

7. **Furniture stays subordinate.** Use the existing chapter navigation, one fixed formula/readout region, kinetic captions, and the current Ultralearn CTA/end-card source. Let blue mean the active mathematical quantity, not the active chapter's entire panel. Use the code's current brand settings, not a captured reference logo or an old render. Typography and layout token values remain the authority in [STYLE.md](../STYLE.md) and [theme.ts](../src/theme.ts); this audit does not remeasure them from zoomed phone captures.

## Limits and review checks

These frames support asset and composition decisions. Sparse stills cannot establish exact animation easing, word-level synchronization, original font identity, or pixel measurements. The contact-sheet sweep provides temporal context, but no listening or new transcription was performed for this audit. Use the recorded narration before changing script timing.

Before accepting the assets, inspect the staircase labels, growing-step comparison, three selected data dots, and right-angle marker at phone scale. Check that every new label arrives with its named object and that stale readouts clear before the next chapter. Inspect the four-picture recap and CTA in a fresh current render. The claim that every SAT question is one of four pictures, and the question ranges, are script-content claims; this visual audit does not establish their completeness or official accuracy.
