## How to read the numbers

- Audience means current subscribers on YouTube and current followers on Instagram, generally rounded. It is not audience at publication or unique people reached. Views can include repeat plays.
- `views_per_current_follower = cumulative public views / current public audience`. A high ratio is useful for finding posts that exceed their channel's current scale. It is not engagement rate, conversion or causal evidence.
- `views_per_retained_median = views / median views of this channel's retained records`, shown only with at least ten records. Full returned Shorts lists, title-screened subsets and twelve loaded Instagram tiles have different coverage. Instagram medians are **loaded-grid comparisons, not normal channel performance**; social-feed YouTube medians describe retained title-screened candidates. Baselines mix ages and round many counters.
- The existing explainer feed also preserves its original rounded-card median; the aggregate median uses the retained counts after selected individual-video enrichments. Small differences are expected, not a second independent measurement.
- Exact-looking K/M expansions are nominal rounded displays; consult `views_precision`, `channel_size_precision` and the source feed. Publication dates remain blank where unavailable. An unknown date is not a recent post.
- Discovery flag `scaled audience outlier` means at least 100,000 views and at least 10x current followers. This is our explicit editorial triage threshold, not a YouTube/Instagram category. `small-base ratio outlier` lacks that absolute scale. Continuous values matter more than the flag.
- Platforms use different counters. Compare within platform and, where useful, within channel. Video age, distribution, growth and subject remain confounders. We have no private retention or learning results.
- A legacy `@MathVisualProof` handle resolves to the renamed Brilliant Chess Studio account with 35 current subscribers. Its historical upload-era identity and size are unknown. Large ratios on those three rows are a denominator warning, not evidence that a 35-subscriber math channel achieved the views with that audience.
- Title-derived `concept_candidate` and `subject_scope` labels are discovery hints, not verified content classifications. Mixed channels contain adjacent physics, computing and interviews; those were not silently promoted into animation exemplars. The aggregate corrects one source-feed classifier collision (`matrix` inside `#codematrixvishal` on an E=mc² title).
