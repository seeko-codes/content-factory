## What the test supports and challenges

The qualitative judgments give a useful hypothesis: **a concrete, low-prerequisite entry can matter more for reach than a high overall explanatory-quality score.** A1 and A3 both scored 12/12, yet their observed counts differ by approximately 97x; C1 and C2 also tie at 12/12. A4 scored lower on earned resolution while taking the largest count in group A. B1 ranked higher for educational explanation but far below B2 in views. The sum of six rubric scores therefore does not produce the observed reach ranking. The reviewer used audience breadth and opening accessibility to break those ties. That additional judgment must not be relabeled a validated numerical predictor.

Keep four outcomes distinct: choice, continuation, satisfaction and learning. The catalogue supplies only cumulative public views. The house quality bar is an acceptance standard; it is not a model that can infer private retention, learner transfer or future distribution from a contact sheet.

A useful next test would lock both selection rules and predictions before collecting outcomes, score a broader prospectively chosen set, compare within channel and common observation windows, and separately assess viewer comprehension. We have not done that experiment. No extra process is required to make the current briefs: use this evidence to choose inspectable constructions and honest promises.

### Evidence behind each row

**A1.** The transcript explicitly promises a formula for a finite geometric sum and states its assumptions. The frames advance from marked powers to individual rectangles, a stacked total, and a boxed formula. A common width and the difference between the endpoint heights connect the summands to the same rectangle's area. The transcript explains that two-way area calculation, so the result is earned. Attention remains on this construction. Its weakness for public reach is a notation-heavy opening and a long setup before a substantial area picture, not a broken contract.

**A2.** The sampled opening spends frames on blank space, branding, and an abstract title before the triangle demonstration. The title names an alternating sum but offers little accessible motivation. Later frames clearly accumulate alternating bands and combine them into a triangular tiling; the visual is doing mathematical work. Small equations and the absence of a transcript leave the exact summand-to-region correspondence less independently legible in this packet. This is mixed evidence for payoff, not a claim that the full video omits an explanation. The visible introductory overhead lowers the attention score.

**A3.** The title promises a visual infinite sum, and the transcript immediately begins a nested-heptagon construction. Successive highlighted regions accompany successive powers of 1/8. The final seven-sector comparison makes 1/7 visually inspectable, and the transcript explicitly connects the two descriptions of the shaded area. The common picture carries both the sequence and its sum. This earns the promised visual reveal, although the construction takes its equal-area partition as a starting fact rather than deriving the required scale factor.

**A4.** The transcript opens with a familiar representation declared invalid and asks what is missing. It then promises a valid four-set alternative. The frames show colored regions tied to set expressions, while the transcript systematically checks the sixteen membership possibilities. This is observable progress and a useful central representation, with attention focused on the sets. E is mixed because the opening challenge about the original diagram is not explicitly resolved in the supplied transcript; the valid replacement is well supported. The sampled original image alone does not justify specifying which unseen intermediate region was discussed or omitted.

**B1.** The opening label states the circle-area subject. The frames show a circle becoming concentric strips and then a triangular form, with r, 2 pi r, and the resulting area calculation supplied. A later dense mathematical/reference panel is substantially harder to inspect and has a different level of notation, which makes attention mixed. **B2.** 'Circle illusion' establishes a credible reveal contract, although the initial audience question is less explicit than a mathematical why-question. ## Why the performance predictions differ from the scores

**A4 > A3 > A1 > A2.** A4's familiar Venn imagery and corrective opening invite people who would not independently seek a sum formula. The viewer gets a recognizable puzzle before specialist notation. A3 has an attractive repeated construction and the appealing contrast of an infinite process with a finite answer, but its particular 1/8 example is narrower. A1 serves a useful and reasonably broad algebra need, yet opens with several symbolic qualifications and takes longer to reach the visible relationship. A2 is the most specialized topic and its sampled opening contains the most branding overhead. These are reach predictions, not deductions from the criterion totals.

Age caveat: A2 was published in August 2021; the other A items are from March-April 2023. Its roughly twenty extra months of exposure could reverse A1/A2 and possibly more. A3 also has about a month's advantage over A1/A4, while A1/A4 are nearly the same age. I retain the listed order because the opening and audience differences appear more consequential, but this is uncertain.

**B2 > B1.** A motion illusion plausibly offers immediate, low-prerequisite curiosity and repeat viewing across a broader audience than an area derivation. The sampled accumulation creates a visible change even before motion can be assessed. B1's circle-area topic is itself broad, and its rearrangement is strong, so this is not an easy prediction. B1's March 2022 publication gives it about thirteen more months than B2's April 2023 publication. The order assumes the movement that makes B2 an illusion actually works in playback; that remains unobserved here.

**C2 > C1.** Both open with a mathematical question and stay on one picture. C2 needs less prerequisite knowledge, uses large colored areas, and turns a familiar school identity into a concrete rearrangement. C1 asks the viewer to coordinate function notation, secant slope, and a limit. That can be educationally valuable while narrowing the immediately accessible audience. C2 is only five days older, so age provides little reason to prefer either. Derivative search demand or packaging could readily overturn this judgment; neither is known from the packet.

## Separate educational-quality order

This means explanatory support for the mathematical relationship, not entertainment or reach. Within groups:

- A: **A1 > A3 > A4 > A2**. A1 explicitly justifies the general formula through two measurements of one area; A3 gives a memorable specific infinite sum; A4 verifies its replacement but leaves the opening correction less explicit; A2's exact correspondence is least inspectable in this packet.
- B: **B1 > B2**. B1 links dimensions and area to a formula. B2 can be an excellent reveal without teaching why the motion behaves that way. Its absent transcript prevents a claim that no explanation exists.
- C: **C2 > C1**, narrowly. C2's subtraction and rearrangement are directly inspectable with fewer prerequisites. C1 presents a useful conceptual-to-formal progression, but the limiting process is harder to verify from separated stills.

A tentative overall educational ordering, if one is required, is **A1 > C2 > A3 > C1 > B1 > A4 > A2 > B2**. Cross-topic and proof-versus-reveal comparisons are weak, so the within-group orders are the more defensible judgments.

## Uncertainties fixed before outcome reveal

- Contact sheets are sampled frames, not continuous videos. No pacing, interpolation, hold duration, smoothness, rewatchability, or missing intermediate event is asserted from the gaps.
- Only A1, A3, and A4 have supplied transcripts. Their text supports content judgments, not voice, music, delivery, or sound quality. All other audio is unassessed.
- Still-image layout and scale may be properties of the supplied sheet. I do not infer the original on-screen readability or exact aspect ratio from the montage alone.
- Actual titles, thumbnails, distribution history, recommendation exposure, channel growth, comments, retention, and outcomes are not available. Equal channel membership does not hold those factors constant.
- Many scores are high, so this packet provides limited score variation. The predictions depend substantially on opening accessibility and audience breadth, not a mechanically summed quality score.
- A4's broad corrective hook, B2's unobserved motion, and A2's age are the largest likely sources of ranking error.
